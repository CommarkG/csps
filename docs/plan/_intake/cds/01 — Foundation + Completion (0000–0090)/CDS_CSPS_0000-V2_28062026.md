# 9. Platform Governor -- Role Definition

The Platform Governor (PG) is the human constitutional authority layer of the CSPS platform. This role was referenced throughout the suite without formal definition. This section closes that gap.
PLATFORM GOVERNOR   The Platform Governor is the only entity that can declare done, approve authority escalation, sign vault entry closures, and make constitutional changes to governing principles. The platform's mechanisms enforce its own protocols -- but they do not self-authorize. The Governor provides the authority that mechanisms cannot provide for themselves.

## 9.1 Who the Platform Governor Is

The Platform Governor is the human architect and owner of the platform -- the person who built, governs, and takes constitutional responsibility for the system. In the context of this suite: Yariv Fink holds the Platform Governor role.
The Governor role is not delegatable to an AI agent. Agents may prepare, propose, and execute -- but every decision that affects the platform's constitutional layer requires the Governor's explicit approval. An AI that acts as Governor without authorization is operating outside its declared authority level.

## 9.4 Governor Unavailability Protocol

When the Governor is unavailable and a Governor-approval decision is required -- the platform does not proceed without approval. It halts at the decision point, declares the halt with reason, and enters a known constraint state until the Governor is available.
Halt Scenario
Platform Behavior
Resumption
GPOW phase gate requires Governor approval
Current phase continues executing within its declared scope. No expansion until approval received.
Governor reviews phase exit state and approves or denies expansion at next available moment.
Agent authority escalation pending
Agent continues at current authority level. All executions at current level are valid.
Governor reviews escalation request and records decision in Governor Decision Registry.
Vault entry awaiting closure declaration
Entry remains active. Propagation continues for open candidates but closure is not declared.
Governor reviews completion evidence and signs closure or requests additional propagation.
