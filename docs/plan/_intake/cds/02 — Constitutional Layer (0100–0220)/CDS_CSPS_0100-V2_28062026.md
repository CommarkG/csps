# 1. The Violation and Its Consequence

It collapsed two distinct mechanisms -- corespine (lineage structure) and pipeline (goal-directed sequence) -- into one, which would cause the entire system to drift continuously. Every document that inherited this phrase must be updated.

# 2. The Canonical Pipeline/Corespine Relationship

These are the only two statements about the pipeline/corespine relationship that are permitted in this suite. All other language is either derived from these two or must be submitted for Governor approval before use.
CORESPINE LAW   STATEMENT 1 -- What they are: A corespine is a governed lineage of purpose -- a family structure with root, trunk, and branches. A pipeline is a declared sequence of stages moving a defined need toward a defined resolution. They are complementary. They are not the same. Neither is a form of the other.
CORESPINE LAW   STATEMENT 2 -- How they relate: A pipeline uses declared segments of one or more ratified corespines. The inheritance and dependencies between a pipeline and its corespines are hardwired and mechanically enforced -- a pipeline stage cannot reference a corespine segment that is not ratified, and a corespine segment cannot be modified without triggering re-validation of every pipeline that uses it.
Most pipelines will traverse segments from multiple corespines. A pipeline may use a small specific part of a corespine trunk, a full branch, a complete corespine, or segments from several corespines simultaneously. The pipeline's goal determines which corespine segments are relevant -- not the corespine's structure.

# 3. Hardwired and Mechanically Enforced -- What This Means

These are not aspirational words. They describe a structural constraint that the platform enforces automatically -- not through human discipline.

'Hardwired' means: the constraint is in the mechanism, not in the instruction. An AI that forgets the rule still cannot violate it because the mechanism rejects the violation before it completes.
'Mechanically enforced' means: the enforcement does not depend on human review, AI compliance, or documentation discipline. It depends on the platform's own structural checks.
