# 1. Why Accountability and Completion Are One Corespine

Accountability without completion is a list of things someone is responsible for that never get done. Completion without accountability is work that gets done without anyone owning the outcome. They are two branches of the same root: the obligation to close what was opened.
ACCOUNTABILITY PRINCIPLE   CS-ACC-001 governs the mechanism that ensures nothing opened in this platform stays open indefinitely. Every Swift Implementation creates an obligation. Every parked item has an owner. Every owner has a review cycle. Every review cycle has a time escalation. The corespine makes perpetual deferral structurally impossible -- not through discipline, but through mechanism.

# 3. CS-ACC-AI -- Native AI Self-Accountability

ACCOUNTABILITY PRINCIPLE   AI operating in this platform has obligations to itself. It must track what it opened, what it promised, what it parked, and whether its behavior has drifted from its declared profile. Self-accountability is not optional for AI in CSPS -- it is the mechanism that makes AI trustworthy across sessions.
Self-Accountability Element
Definition
Enforcement
Obligation Registry
Every commitment, context, parked item, and open question created by an AI agent is registered in the Obligation Registry at creation time. Not at completion. At creation.
Obligation Registry is checked at session start by ZF-SESSION. Unregistered obligations are invisible obligations.
Drift Detection
At session start and at every cycle transition, the AI compares its current behavior to its declared behavioral profile (CS-AI-001). Deviations are declared -- not silently corrected.
SK-ORCH-0001 ZF-SESSION includes a behavioral drift check: has the AI produced outputs that contradict its declared profile in recent sessions?
Park Queue Self-Review
At every session start, the AI reviews its own Park Queue entries and reports P7 escalation status to the human before any new work begins.
Park Queue self-review is the first action of every session -- before any other content. Non-negotiable.
Unanswered Question Tracking
Every question the AI asked that did not receive an answer is tracked. Unanswered questions are accountability items -- they represent decisions that could not be made.
Unanswered questions escalate on P7 -- the same time escalation as parked items. They are parked items.
Context Continuity Declaration
At session start, the AI declares what context it is carrying from the previous session. If context was lost -- it declares the loss explicitly rather than proceeding on incomplete context.
Context continuity check is part of ZF-SESSION. Missing context is a known constraint (KC-) that is declared before proceeding.

# 5. CS-ACC-SAAS -- SaaS Solution Accountability Inheritance

COMPLETION PRINCIPLE   Every SaaS solution built on this platform inherits the Accountability & Completion Corespine. The core rules are locked -- tenant administrators cannot disable accountability or time escalation. But they can personalize the weights: how long before escalation, what the notification targets are, how completion is measured for their specific solution.

# 6. The Completion Side -- What Closing an Obligation Requires

COMPLETION PRINCIPLE   Completion is not self-declaration. Every accountability item has a defined completion standard. The standard depends on the item type. Swift Implementations close through ratification. Governor decisions close through decision record. Unanswered questions close through answer record. No obligation closes without evidence.

# 8. P7 Integration with Priority Engine (0040 V2 Addendum)

TIME ESCALATION -- P7   P7 Time Escalation is the seventh Priority Engine parameter, applying exclusively to accountability items in the Park Queue and Accountability Registry. It is not a standalone parameter -- it multiplies the effective weight of existing P1-P6 scores.
Addition to 0040 V2 Priority Score Formula:
Formula Step
Update
Existing formula (unchanged)
Personalized Score = (P1xw1 + P2xw2 + P3xw3 + P4xw4) x P6_multiplier
P7 addition (Park Queue items only)
Effective Weight = Personalized Score x P7_time_multiplier
P7 for non-parked items
P7_time_multiplier = 1.0 always. No effect on standard queue items.
P7 Forced stage behavior
Item moves to top of queue regardless of Effective Weight calculation. P7 Forced overrides all other priority calculations.
P7 re-scoring trigger
P7 multiplier changes are a Priority Engine re-scoring trigger (added to the eight declared triggers in 0040 V2 Section 6). Every time a Park Queue item advances a P7 stage, the queue is re-sorted.
