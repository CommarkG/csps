# 1. Position in the System

Inherits from:  0000 (Foundation), 0010 (Depth & Modularity), 1030 (Gap Detection). All governing principles apply.
Relationship to other protocols:  This protocol receives from all others -- and feeds back into all others. It is the only protocol in the suite with bidirectional relationships to every layer.
When this runs:  Continuously. Not at a single gate. Dynamic Completion is a living loop -- always receiving, always analyzing, always propagating.
Wiring State:  Defined. Becomes Wired when the vault receives its first entry and the first propagation analysis is completed.
Dynamic Completion is the mechanism that closes the most expensive gap in platform development: the gap between what was improved and what was updated. It is the answer to the universal failure where platform element 15 introduces a significant innovation and elements 1 through 14 are left behind -- quietly, permanently, and at increasing cost.
An improvement that stays where it was found is a missed opportunity. An improvement that propagates becomes a platform upgrade. Dynamic Completion makes propagation mandatory -- not aspirational.
ZERO FINDINGS MANDATORY   Before any propagation analysis -- check what already exists in the vault. A finding that was already received, analyzed, and propagated must not be re-processed as a new entry. Zero Findings applies to the vault itself -- the vault must not accumulate duplicates.

# 2. The Problem This Protocol Solves

Platforms evolve. Elements are improved. Bugs are fixed. Innovations are introduced. And almost universally -- the improvement is applied where the problem was found, and nowhere else.
The reason is not negligence. It is architecture. There is no mechanism that asks: where else does this apply? There is no system that tracks what exists, what was improved, and what has not yet received the benefit of that improvement. The question is never asked because asking it is not enforced.
A platform where element 15 is at innovation level and elements 1 through 14 are at launch level is not a platform. It is 15 separate implementations wearing the same name. Consistency is the product. Inconsistency is the failure.
This protocol enforces the question. Every improvement, every gap resolution, every error fix, every innovation -- is received by the vault, analyzed for propagation candidates, and tracked until it has been applied everywhere it applies or explicitly declared as not applicable with a reason.
ZERO FINDINGS MANDATORY   The most dangerous assumption in platform development is that what exists is current. Before propagating an improvement -- check the current state of every candidate element. Some may have already received the improvement through an adjacent propagation. Some may be at a wiring state that prevents them from receiving it. Check first -- always.

# 3. The Dynamic Completion Vault

The vault is the central registry of all improvements, fixes, innovations, and gap resolutions that have been identified anywhere in the platform. It is not a log. It is not a backlog. It is a living analysis engine -- every entry triggers a propagation analysis, and that analysis produces a propagation plan that is tracked to completion.
Nothing enters the vault and waits passively. Every vault entry is active -- it has a propagation status, a candidate list, and a completion target. A vault entry is closed only when every candidate has received the improvement or been explicitly declared exempt with a reason.

# 4. The Propagation Analysis

Propagation analysis is the process that converts a vault entry into a propagation plan. It runs on every entry -- immediately upon receipt. It asks one question above all others:
Where else in this platform does this improvement apply -- and what is the evidence that it does not apply to everything it has not yet reached?

# 5. The Propagation Loop -- The Living Mechanism

Dynamic Completion is not a one-time analysis. It is a continuous loop. The loop has four phases that repeat for every active vault entry until the entry is closed.
Phase
Name
What Happens
Trigger for Next Phase
1
Receive
Vault receives entry. Entry is structured and validated. Propagation analysis is initiated.
Entry is complete and unique.
2
Analyze
Propagation analysis runs five steps. Candidate list is produced. Priority is assigned. Depth levels are identified.
Propagation plan is approved by Platform Governor.
3
Propagate
Each candidate receives the improvement at the declared depth level. Wiring states are updated. Backpacks are revised. Audit mirrors are updated.
All candidates are marked Completed or Exempt with documented reason.
4
Close
Closure declaration is made. Full audit trail is recorded. Entry is archived -- not deleted. Platform Governor signs closure.
All candidates addressed. No open investigations remaining.
The loop closes only when every candidate is addressed. A partially propagated improvement is an open loop. Open loops accumulate. An accumulated platform of open loops is a platform heading toward structural incoherence.

## 5.1 Loop Integrity Rules

Rule
Definition
No silent phase transitions
Every phase transition is declared. The vault records when an entry moved from Receive to Analyze, Analyze to Propagate, Propagate to Close.
No open investigations beyond one cycle
An entry in Requires Investigation status must be resolved within the current development cycle. If it cannot be resolved, it must be escalated to the Platform Governor with a declared reason.
No self-referential exemptions
An element cannot exempt itself from receiving an improvement that originates from its own corespine family without Platform Governor approval.
Wiring state must be updated on receipt
When a candidate receives a propagated improvement, its wiring state must be re-evaluated. An improvement does not automatically elevate wiring state -- it must be verified.
Zero Findings before Close
Before closing an entry -- run Zero Findings on the candidate list. Confirm no new elements have been added to the platform since the analysis was run that would qualify as candidates.
DYNAMIC COMPLETION   The propagation loop itself is subject to Dynamic Completion. If the loop mechanism is improved -- that improvement is entered into the vault and propagated to all active entries currently in the loop. The loop improves itself.

# 6. Depth-Aware Propagation

Propagation is not one-size-fits-all. An improvement at Full depth does not necessarily require Full depth updates in all candidates. The propagation plan must be depth-specific -- matching the improvement to the depth level where it applies in each candidate.

Propagation to Core Candidates
Propagation to Medium Candidates
Propagation to Full Candidates

Mandatory -- Core is the foundation. A Core improvement affects all depth levels.
Mandatory -- Medium inherits Core. Core improvement changes the foundation of Medium.
Mandatory -- Full inherits Core and Medium. Core improvement changes the foundation of Full.
MEDIUM improvement
Not required -- unless the improvement reveals a Core inadequacy.
Mandatory -- Medium improvement propagates to all Medium-built candidates.

The improvement must wait until the candidate is built to Medium -- or the candidate must be flagged for depth progression as a prerequisite to receiving the improvement.

# 8. The Preservation-Learning-Evolving Loop

Dynamic Completion is built on three interlocked mechanisms. Together they form the loop that keeps the platform coherent as it grows.

## 8.1 Preservation

Nothing that was built correctly is lost. Every validated element, every resolved gap, every approved governance decision is preserved in the vault's audit trail. The platform never forgets what it decided and why.
Preservation is not archiving. It is active availability. A preserved decision can be retrieved, referenced, and applied to new situations without being rebuilt from scratch.
ZERO FINDINGS MANDATORY   Preservation requires Zero Findings before every new build. What exists must be found before anything new is created. The vault is the memory of the platform. It must be consulted -- not bypassed.

## 8.2 Learning

Every vault entry is a lesson. Gap Type 1 appearing repeatedly in the same corespine family is a lesson about how that corespine is being defined. Wiring State Mismatches clustering around a specific pipeline stage is a lesson about how that stage's inheritance is being declared.
The vault does not just store findings -- it patterns them. After every propagation cycle, the vault produces a Pattern Report: which gap types appeared most frequently, which corespine families generated the most entries, which depth levels are most stagnant.
Pattern Type
What It Reveals
Response
Recurring Gap Type in same corespine family
The corespine definition has a structural weakness that keeps producing the same gap.
Escalate to corespine definition review. Update 1020 and propagate.
Wiring State Mismatches clustering around one pipeline stage
That stage's inheritance declaration is inadequate or its upstream sources are unreliable.
Escalate to pipeline definition review. Update 1010 and propagate.
Depth stagnation across multiple elements
The platform is not progressing depth levels systematically. Build cycles are completing Core but not advancing to Medium.
Escalate to Platform Governor. Depth progression must be scheduled explicitly.
Repeated orphan stages in new pipelines
Goal Definition Q1 is not being applied rigorously. Stages are being added without goal traceability.
Escalate to Wizard Architecture (2000). Goal Definition wizard must be strengthened.
Innovation entries without propagation completion
Improvements are being introduced but the loop is not closing. Propagation is stalling.
Platform Governor review. Propagation targets may be under-resourced or structurally blocked.

## 8.3 Evolving

The platform evolves through deliberate propagation -- not through organic drift. Every improvement is a controlled evolution event. It is received, analyzed, propagated to candidates, verified, and closed. The platform at the end of a cycle is demonstrably more complete than at the start -- and the evidence of that completeness lives in the vault.
Evolution without documentation is drift. Evolution with documentation is governance. The vault is the difference between the two.
DYNAMIC COMPLETION   The Preservation-Learning-Evolving loop is the highest-level mechanism in the Dynamic Completion Protocol. It is the mechanism that makes platform growth sustainable. When the loop is functioning -- the platform gets better with every cycle. When the loop breaks -- the platform accumulates debt silently. Maintaining the loop is the Platform Governor's primary structural responsibility.
