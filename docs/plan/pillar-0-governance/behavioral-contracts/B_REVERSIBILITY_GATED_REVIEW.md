## B_REVERSIBILITY_GATED_REVIEW — review depth scales with reversibility cost (S063)

**Canonical wording:**

> Review gate tier is determined by reversibility cost and novelty, NOT by step count or default Opus-review habit. Three tiers, strictly applied:
>
> **AUTO-EXECUTE** (no Opus gate): single-file edit OR matches existing pattern OR clear DONE criteria AND reversible without data loss. Sonnet acts, commits, reports 3-line update to council file. Governor relays only when complete.
>
> **CHECK-IN GATE** (pause for Opus glance, NOT full ADVANCE): borderline triage classification OR mass commit (>10 files, dry-run shown first) OR novel pattern not in existing templates. Write "ASK OPUS: <specific question>" to sonnet-turn.md, keep working on parallel items.
>
> **FULL ADVANCE GATE** (stop, full Opus review required): ratification moment OR PROTO completion claim (per gap_DONE_CLAIM discipline) OR cross-spine impact (GVRN+ARCH+VALD simultaneously) OR new FINDING K≥2 not in register OR milestone declaration.

**Counterweight:**

> Do not use AUTO-EXECUTE for anything that has never been done before in CSPS. "Matches established pattern" means: a validator/hook of identical structure exists, the DONE criteria are identical, and the only novelty is the name/content. If ANY of those differ meaningfully, escalate to CHECK-IN GATE.

**Source:** S062 meta-critique by Governor + Opus-10. IMPROVEMENT-S062-RELAY-OPTIMIZATION. Three-tier model proved in S063 items 1+2+3: 8 commits landed with 0 mid-mandate ADVANCE cycles (was ~17 in S062).

**Why this matters:**

S062 had 17 relay cycles for work that was reversible single-file edits. Each relay = ~2-3 turns + Governor bridge cost. At 3× overhead, a session that could have been 15 turns took 45 turns. The asymmetry: reversible work penalized as heavily as irreversible work. The fix: reviewers are expensive — reserve full review for irreversible, complex, or novel work. Rubber-stamp for established patterns.

**Anti-patterns (NEVER do):**
- Asking for ADVANCE on every step regardless of reversibility
- Treating "novel to me in this session" as "novel to the platform" (check existing patterns first)
- Escalating to FULL ADVANCE when a single targeted question would resolve the uncertainty
