7 sections (in order)

  • §1 JTBD — who, what, why
  • §2 Kill condition — when is this dead?
  • §3 EPOCH scope — what ships in v1?
  • §4 Connection map — integrations

Why this gate exists

PMI = 5/5 is not bureaucracy. It's proof you know what you're building before you touch code. Every section that stays vague = a redesign waiting to happen.

Opus role

  • Challenges each section
  • Prevents false PMI scores
  • Designs PROTO for fork

Open architectural questions (OPUS-6 perspective)

Phase 2 weight: Is the 7-section wizard the right entry point for external developers, or is it CSPS-internal? The planning depth required may be appropriate for the Governor + AI council relay model, but needs design review if external developers are the audience.

Phase 3 trust: "Touch nothing in the inherited layer" is easy to say but hard to enforce. The DNA block system + 140 validators are the mechanical answer — but the developer experience of "I want to customize auth" needs a designed response: "Fork the relevant lib, not the .claude config."

Phase 6 loop: CAIE extraction (BATCH-J) would close this loop by feeding what Phase 6 learns back into the platform. Currently Phase 6 is a dead end — evaluation results have no feed-forward mechanism.
